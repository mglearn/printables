Provenance

Teachers Give Teachers grew out of TCEA's professional-learning work as a free, no-checkout companion to its programs. It is organized as a hub of collections:

  • Student Refusal Forms — meme, gaming, and content-area reflection sheets for the moment a reminder won't help. Their design connects to the ACE framework (Articulate, Connect, Extend) and to Visible Learning research on evaluation and reflection, adapted from TCEA professional-development materials.
  • Getting to Know You — subject-based interest-inventory worksheets for Grades K–12 in English, Spanish, and Vietnamese, published separately at mglearn.github.io/gtky with its own source list.

Content, code, artwork, and layout are produced with the help of generative-AI tools and refined under Miguel Guhlin's direction and editorial review; a human stays in the loop on every decision. Any translations are machine-assisted and flagged for local educator review before classroom use.

Privacy

Every page runs entirely in the browser. There are no logins and no accounts, nothing is uploaded, and no personal data is collected or transmitted. Downloads are ordinary PNG and PDF files.
