Text-specific discussion questions (open — more than one answer)

  • Predict: Jonas is chosen for a unique, “honored” role — what might the community be hiding behind Sameness?
  • Question: Why might the community trade memory and color for “Sameness”?
  • Clarify: What does “release” turn out to mean, and how does it change Jonas?
  • Summarize: Trace what Jonas gains and loses as he receives memories, without opinion.
  • Interpret the theme: How does Jonas’s awakening reveal a theme about memory and choice? Use two details (paraphrase).
Optional Jigsaw lenses (p. 4): Character (Jonas and the Giver) · Setting (the engineered community) · Theme (memory/individuality vs. Sameness) · Plot (the decision to leave) · Symbol (color and the sled).
