Text-specific discussion questions (open — more than one answer)

  • Predict: A sour, lonely Mary arrives at a house of secrets — what might the locked garden change in her?
  • Question: Why might Burnett tie the garden’s revival to the children’s health?
  • Clarify: What does “Magic” mean to Colin — supernatural, or something else?
  • Summarize: Trace Mary’s change from the opening to the garden’s bloom, without opinion.
  • Interpret the theme: How do the garden and the friendships reveal a theme about renewal or belonging? Use two details.
Optional Jigsaw lenses (p. 4): Character (Mary and Colin) · Symbol (the garden) · Theme (renewal and nature) · Plot (secrets revealed) · Context (Edwardian England — preview colonial attitudes).

Sample 3–2–1 notes: Aida, symbol lens

Name: Aida · Home group: Priya’s group · Period __ · Meetings ___ & ___ · Section: “The Secret Garden,” Chunk 2 · Expert lens: Symbol

What a finished prep looks like. Kit section 6 / Organizer A completed for one reader, so students can compare their notes to a strong model.

Questions

Is the “Magic” nature, effort, or belief?

Who is healed more — Mary or Colin?

3 · Important ideas

1. Mary finds a hidden, neglected garden and begins to tend it.

2. Caring for it changes her mood and her health.

3. Colin, believed a hopeless invalid, grows stronger outdoors.

Vocabulary

contrary = stubbornly opposed.

moor = open, rolling upland.

2 · Supporting details

1. The first green shoots appear (mid-novel). → Mirror Mary’s warming heart.

2. Colin stands and walks in the garden (late). → Shows the garden’s healing effect.

1 · Summary in my own words

A neglected girl restores a locked garden and, with it, herself and a sickly boy.

Bring to the circle

My interpretation: Care for living things heals the one who gives the care.
Synthesis (two lenses): The garden (symbol) embodies renewal (theme): as it revives, so do Mary and Colin. Text points: the first shoots (Chunk 2) and Colin walking (Chunk 3).
After discussion: My thinking became clearer once the group compared evidence.

TEKS evidence: 6(C,D) supported inference; 7(A) theme through characters/events; 1(D) response to a peer.
