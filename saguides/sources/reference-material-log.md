# State-Supplied Reference Material Log

Per spec section 10 (Science Formula Triangles) and section 18. A science
formula-triangle candidate may be created **only** when the relationship appears
on the current state-supplied science reference materials for the relevant
assessment.

**Build date:** 2026-09-25 · **Testing year targeted:** 2025–2026

---

## STAAR Grade 8 Science reference materials

Source:
https://www.texasassessment.gov/resources/reference-materials/staar-grade-8-science-reference-materials

### Formulas on the Grade 8 science sheet → triangle candidates

| Relationship | Form | Variables for triangle |
|---|---|---|
| Density | D = m / V | m (top), D, V (bottom) |
| Average speed | s = d / t | d (top), s, t (bottom) |
| Net force | F = m·a | F (top), m, a (bottom) |
| Work | W = F·d | W (top), F, d (bottom) |

Also on the sheet: **Periodic Table of the Elements** (not a triangle candidate).

### Classroom formula triangles NOT on the sheet → fail-closed

These appeared as classroom formula-triangle cue cards but are **not** on the
Grade 8 science reference sheet, so per sections 10/26 they are **CLASSROOM USE
ONLY** (no test-use triangle built):

- Weight (W = m·g)
- Momentum (p = m·v)
- Pressure (P = F/A)
- Power (P = W/t)
- Wave speed (v = f·λ)
- Ohm's law (V = I·R)

If a future sheet adds any of these, build the triangle and move the row to
`CANDIDATE` in the compliance matrix.

## STAAR Grade 5 Science reference materials

No formula suitable for a triangle is used from grade 5 in this build. **No
grade-5 science formula triangles created** (fail-closed).

## STAAR Mathematics reference materials (grades 6–8, Algebra I)

Mathematics reference materials exist, **but per section 7 a formula's presence on
a math reference sheet does not make a locally created formula aid allowable.** No
math formula sheets or formula triangles were created. Math candidates are limited
to the explicitly permitted categories (number charts, place-value charts,
fraction models, geometric figures, mnemonics, blank graphic organizers).
