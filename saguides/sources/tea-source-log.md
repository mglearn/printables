## Governing framework

- Supplemental aids are **locally-approved designated supports**.
- They **do not require TEA approval**; **TEA does not review or approve locally
  created supplemental aids**.
- Eligibility is student-specific: the student must **routinely, independently,
  and effectively** use the support during classroom instruction and testing.
- Decisions rest with local teams (campus support / §504 / ARD) and the district
  or campus testing coordinator.
