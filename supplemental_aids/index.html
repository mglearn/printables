The four strands

Each sheet is a single printable page of visual cue cards for one TEKS strand — start with the overview, then the strand you're teaching. The count updates from the manifest.

—

Five ways to use them

1. Reinforce Matter & Energy — particle models make abstract ideas visible. 2. Make Force & Motion visual — formula triangles as quick retrieval cues. 3. Give Earth & Space a picture — a diagram sticks when a paragraph doesn't. 4. Connect living systems — cells, photosynthesis, food webs for stations and intervention. 5. Build the cue before test day — so the printable is a familiar friend, not a surprise. The visual should help students retrieve a relationship they already understand — not replace the understanding.
