Five ways to use them

  1. Reinforce Matter & Energy — particle models make abstract ideas visible.
  2. Make Force & Motion visual — formula triangles as quick retrieval cues.
  3. Give Earth & Space a picture — a diagram sticks when a paragraph doesn't.
  4. Connect living systems — cells, photosynthesis, food webs for stations and intervention.
  5. Build the cue before test day — so the printable is a familiar friend, not a surprise.

The visual should help students retrieve a relationship they already understand — not replace the understanding.
