Decimals & Place Value

Use the cue, then fade the cue. Study the cue card for 60 seconds. Then cover it before answering Round 1. Check the card only after you commit to an answer.

Cue focus  The places to the right of the decimal point name part of a whole: tenths, hundredths, thousandths. We see them in money and measurement. A decimal can be written as a fraction: 0.5 = 5/10 (five tenths). Money uses hundredths: $0.25 = 25 hundredths of a dollar. Each decimal place is 10× the place to its right, just like whole numbers.

Round 1 — Retrieve It

  1. Name the first three places to the right of the decimal point.
  2. Write 0.5 as a fraction.
  3. $0.25 is how many hundredths of a dollar?
  4. Do decimal places name whole numbers or parts of a whole?

Round 2 — Sketch It

Draw a 10×10 grid (100 squares). Shade squares to show 0.25. Write the decimal and the fraction it equals.

Decimals & Place Value — Apply It

Keep the cue card covered. Answer first. Then use the cue to check, revise, and explain what you changed.

Round 3 — Apply the Idea

  1. Write 0.7 as a fraction. Which place is the 7 in?
  2. A pencil is 0.03 meter thick. How many hundredths of a meter is that?
  3. Write $1.50 in words as dollars and cents. What place is the 5 in?

Round 4 — Reason from Evidence

Sam says 0.5 is less than 0.25 because 5 is less than 25. Is he correct? Use a grid or fractions to explain which decimal is greater.

Decimals & Place Value — ACE It

Use the cue card only if you need it. The goal is to show what you can explain, connect, and use on your own.

A

Articulate

Explain what tenths and hundredths mean. Use the words part, whole, and decimal point.

C

Connect

Connect decimals to money. Show how $0.25 uses the hundredths place and equals 25 hundredths of a dollar.

E

Extend

Write a decimal that has a digit in the thousandths place. Tell what part of a whole that digit names.

Confidence check. Circle one after you finish: I still need the cue / I need it once in a while / I can explain this without it.

Teacher Key — Decimals & Place Value

Remove this page before student copying. Answers below give expected ideas, not required wording.

Retrieve It

  1. Tenths, hundredths, thousandths.
  2. 5/10 (which also equals 1/2).
  3. 25 hundredths.
  4. Parts of a whole.

Sketch It — look for

A 10×10 grid with 25 of the 100 squares shaded. Labeled 0.25 = 25/100 (also 1/4).

Apply It

  1. 7/10. The 7 is in the tenths place.
  2. 3 hundredths of a meter.
  3. One dollar and fifty cents. The 5 is in the tenths place (5 dimes).

Reason from Evidence — look for

No. 0.5 = 50/100 and 0.25 = 25/100, so 0.5 is greater. On a grid, 0.5 shades 50 squares while 0.25 shades only 25.

ACE — Extend look-for

Any decimal with a thousandths digit, such as 0.004, where the student says it names 4 thousandths (4 parts out of 1,000) of a whole.
