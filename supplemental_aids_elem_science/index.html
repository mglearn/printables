Five ways to use them

  1. Spiral grades 3–5 — build the vocabulary and pictures early so grade 5 review is recall, not re-teach.
  2. Make abstract ideas visible — the water cycle, circuits, and food chains as one clear diagram.
  3. Anchor each reporting category — a picture sticks when a paragraph doesn't.
  4. Support intervention & stations — one card per concept for small groups and tutoring.
  5. Build the cue before test day — so the printable is a familiar friend, not a surprise.

The visual should help students retrieve an idea they already understand — not replace the understanding.
