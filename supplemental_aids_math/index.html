Classroom and Review Resource

These visual cue cards are designed primarily for classroom instruction, intervention, retrieval practice, and review. They should not be assumed to qualify as supplemental aids during a STAAR assessment.

Texas has specific requirements governing supplemental aids used as designated supports. Student eligibility, routine use, documentation, the format of the aid, and local approval requirements must all be considered.

Before using any resource from this collection during a state assessment, review the current Texas Education Agency accessibility guidance and consult your district or campus testing coordinator.

Meeting the format requirements for a supplemental aid does not make a resource automatically allowable for every student.

Five ways to use them

  1. Anchor the rule — integer signs and order of operations become a picture, not a paragraph.
  2. Make proportional reasoning visual — ratios, unit rate, and percent bars as quick retrieval cues.
  3. Keep the reference handy — one page of measurement formulas for problem solving and classroom review.
  4. Support intervention & stations — one card per skill for small groups, tutoring, and warm-ups.
  5. Build the cue during instruction — introduce visual cues during teaching, retrieval practice, intervention, and classroom assessment so students learn to use them independently.

The visual should help students retrieve a relationship they already understand — not replace the understanding. Routine classroom use alone does not make a resource allowable during STAAR: test-day use is a separate decision governed by current TEA eligibility, documentation, format, and local approval requirements.
